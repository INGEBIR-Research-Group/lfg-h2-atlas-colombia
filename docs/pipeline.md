# Modeling choices and the scripts that implement them

- **Site catalog** (`scripts/02_site_catalog.py`). Registry rows are harmonized into canonical sites by name normalization, explicit overrides for the large regional sites (`inputs/site_overrides.csv`), row-level fixes (`inputs/site_row_fixes.csv`) and a dominant-code rule, whereby a name filed under several municipal codes of one department goes to the code holding at least 70 % of its mass. Status is active or closed in 2024; the licensed end of life is the 2024 registry date, and the remaining life is counted from 1 January 2026.
- **Scope.** The article covers the 248 sites active in 2024. The model runs every one of the 1,868 catalog sites, and `results/site_results.csv` keeps them all, but the national aggregates of `results/mc_national.csv` and the values of `results/numbers_results.csv` (except the registry inventory) cover the active sites only.
- **Deposits** (`scripts/03_build_waste.py`). Back-cast from the first three observed years and projected from the per-capita disposal of 2020 to 2024, with population held constant after 2042. Waste deposited before a site opened is excluded, and every projected deposit is split at the licensed date into a licensed and a beyond-license layer.
- **Composition** (`scripts/04_build_composition.py`). The measured characterization of the municipality where its PGIRS reports one, and otherwise the UPME departmental profile (see `docs/composition.md`).
- **Methane and collection** (`scripts/11_lfg_h2_model.py`). Four decay categories and five precipitation zones of the Colombia Landfill Gas Model. Each year's deposit is split into ten parts placed at the middle of their tenth of the year, each starts to generate after a lag of half a year, and the rate is read at mid-year (`decay_kernel`). The collection efficiency of an engineered field at a sanitary landfill is triangular (0.41, 0.60, 0.71), with one quantile shared by all sites; `scripts/20_variants.py` adds an efficiency by cover stage for each annual deposit. The existing destruction is metered at Doña Juana, inspected at La Pradera and Presidente, and assumed at Nuevo Mondoñedo (`inputs/site_facts.csv`).
- **Route A.** Overall reformer efficiency interpolated in the logarithm of the design flow between 0.60 at 1.5 t/d and 0.76 at 216 t/d; PSA recovery interpolated in the same way; fuel-to-feed ratio from Equation 32 of the article, which includes that recovery; auxiliary power from the least-cost LFGcost-Web generator; methane slip of engines, upgrading units, reformer furnace and capture boiler counted. A conservative bound at E = 0.35 is evaluated alongside.
- **Route B.** Least-cost feasible generator (30 kW microturbine floor) and an alkaline electrolyzer.
- **Variants within the main ensemble.** Incremental gas at the four sites with existing destruction, diversion of the two fastest decay categories (5 % in 2026 rising to 50 % in 2035), and compression of electrolytic hydrogen from 0.1 to 3 MPa; their national results are columns of `results/mc_national.csv`.
- **Capture variant.** Only the concentrated process CO2 is credited, with capture heat from a boiler burning recovered gas, capture power from the auxiliary generator, transport and storage at 40 (20–80) USD/t and their emissions deducted.
- **Costs.** Discounted cash flow over 2026 to 2040, with all capital in 2023 USD through the chemical engineering plant cost index. The reformer capital comes from the pooled, scope-harmonized fit of `scripts/08_capex_regression.py` (exponent 0.806, level 5.15 million USD at 1,500 kg/d, lognormal residual sigma 0.532), drawn once for all sites; `scripts/11_lfg_h2_model.py NDRAW --split` repeats the ensemble with a common level and an independent residual per site.
- **Further variants** (`scripts/20_variants.py`). Generation 22 % lower; minimum loads of 50 % or of 30 % and 25 %; design at the 75th percentile or at the maximum of the annual flows; two modular trains; collection efficiency by cover stage; collection efficiency independent or correlated at 0.5 between sites; composition error common to each department; reformer capital from the predictive distribution or from a mixed model; methane at its netback value as electricity; capture fractions of 0.85 and 0.95, without transport and storage emissions, and with 1 % or 10 % of the stored CO2 lost; cleanup cost and electricity multiplied; and Route B with the generator fixed at its modal technology.
